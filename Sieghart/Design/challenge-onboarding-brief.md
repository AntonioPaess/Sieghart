# Challenge onboarding — three-minute product brief

October 8, 2026. Requested by the user. **The user will supply the storytelling; this is the experience brief, not the final script or a completed judging flow.**

## Goal

A judge should understand the companion's purpose, feel a distinctive expressive interaction and complete a useful action within three minutes. Show the strongest experience first; do not turn the opening into a tour of every settings page. The full macOS utility catalog remains accessible afterwards.

## Proposed sequence, subject to the user's story

| Time budget | User action | Value demonstrated |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–20 s | Meet the arriving companion; skip/reduced-motion equivalent | A personal, expressive companion with a clear purpose |
| 20–40 s | Choose one of the six avatars | Visible choice and immediate identity across the app/island |
| 40–90 s | Try a short focus interaction; pause/resume without waiting a full session | A calmer next step, an understandable state and a relevant avatar reaction |
| 90–130 s | Try a clearly labeled local clipboard sample or a provided file | Recover something useful without account/network setup |
| 130–160 s | Give a command or use its accessible button equivalent | Listening/thinking/result states only when that capability exists |
| 160–180 s | Reveal the island and its few chosen tools | The companion fits into the workflow and the user controls its behavior |

Permission, account, real clipboard, microphone and Mac-only sensor setup must not be prerequisites for the judging path. Any demonstration data is explicitly labeled; it never appears as real spending, hardware readings or a user's actual clipboard. AI prices, email, uploads and MCP cannot be required online steps. Voice needs an offline accessible equivalent. Real tools use their existing opt-in path afterwards.

## Acceptance before Challenge packaging

- Time the complete story from a clean install; repeat with permissions declined and without network access.
- Make the main benefit clear without requiring the judge to discover hidden menus or read the entire roadmap.
- Provide Skip/Back/replay and Reduce Motion equivalents for the arrival; interruptions leave coherent state.
- Every promised action must exist in the submitted target. Planned specialist agents/email/health integrations stay out of claims until implemented.
- Validate the actual submission platform/requirements separately; the macOS development target is not proof of Challenge compatibility.
- Final wording, emotional arc and which capabilities to feature await the user's storytelling.
